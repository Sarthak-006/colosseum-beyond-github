# Solana / crypto X growth mechanics

Platform behavior changes; principles below focus on **distribution mechanics** that tend to persist.

## Replies vs quote-tweets (QT)

| Tool | Best for |
| ---- | -------- |
| Reply | Adding data, a working mini-demo, correcting a technical claim, thanking with substance. |
| QT | Reframing a news item with your project's lens, attaching a clip, "here is how we solved X" with visual. |

**Rule of thumb:** reply when you add to **their** thread; QT when you need **your** frame visible on timelines.

## Threads

- **First tweet** is the entire pitch: outcome + for whom + proof pointer.
- **3–7 tweets** beats 20 unless deep technical tutorial.
- End with one CTA: try demo, follow for part 2, or feedback prompt.

## Cadence (directional)

- Early stage: enough presence to stay in memory (often **several** touches/week if sustainable), weighted to **one strong** demo or proof post over many low-signal posts.
- Prefer **scheduled** deep work blocks so posting does not cannibalize ship speed.

## Spaces

- Prepare **3 anchor stories** (problem, aha moment, why Solana).
- Have **link in bio** or pinned with demo before going live.
- Clip **30–60s** moments for async distribution.

## Visuals

- Screen recordings with **cursor + voice** outperform static screenshots for new tools.
- Before/after or "old way vs this way" is highly legible.

## What to avoid

- Engagement bait unrelated to product.
- Buying followers or fake metrics (ecosystem participants notice).
- Arguing with trolls when brand is early and fragile.
